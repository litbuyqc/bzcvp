01

Choose a use case

Decide what you need: daily sneakers, a warm layer, a matching set or a finishing accessory. Browse the corresponding category.

02

Record the details

Save the product URL, seller, selected color, measurements and quoted price. Note anything the listing leaves unclear.

03

Plan your QC checks

Where available, inspect actual-item photos, stitching, labels and measurements. Ask for clarification while returns are still possible.

04

Account for delivery, service fees, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties. Check return conditions before committing.

04 / Questions, answered

Before you
build your list.

Short answers for shoppers researching an Oopbuy spreadsheet, QC photos and category-based fashion discovery.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a shopping research list used to organize product links, prices, sizes and comparison notes. It may be a literal spreadsheet or a category-based directory. A listing alone does not verify product quality, authenticity or agent acceptance.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial discovery page with links to CAFANS. It is not operated by Oopbuy and does not represent the brands mentioned.

Where do the category links go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing buttons open the CAFANS homepage. This page does not host a downloadable spreadsheet or process orders.

How should I compare sizing and QC photos?

Compare the seller's measurements with an item you already own. Where QC photos are available, inspect the actual item, labels, stitching, material appearance and measurements. Photos have limits and do not independently establish authenticity.

Does a listed price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check the product price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties before payment. Confirm current charges with the relevant seller or service.
